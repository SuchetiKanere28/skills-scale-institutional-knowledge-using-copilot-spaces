# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## Stakeholder Engagement Lead

### Role Summary
Stakeholder Engagement Leads manage communication and relationships with external and internal stakeholders. They ensure alignment on expectations, gather feedback, and maintain transparent communication throughout the project lifecycle.

### Responsibilities
- Manage communication with external stakeholders and key decision-makers
- Gather and consolidate stakeholder feedback and requirements
- Ensure stakeholder alignment on project goals, timelines, and deliverables
- Provide stakeholder-focused status updates and reporting
- Escalate concerns and manage stakeholder expectations
- Coordinate stakeholder engagement activities and reviews

### Goals
- Maintain strong stakeholder relationships and trust
- Ensure project decisions align with stakeholder needs
- Minimize stakeholder-related risks and surprises
- Drive project acceptance and adoption

### Interactions with Existing Roles
- **Works with Project Manager**: Coordinates on status updates, escalation protocols, and project milestones
- **Works with Product Manager**: Aligns on product vision and value proposition for stakeholders
- **Works with Technical Lead**: Translates technical decisions for stakeholder communication
- **Supports Developers**: Provides stakeholder context and requirements clarification

### Typical Communication
- Stakeholder briefings and governance reviews
- Feedback summaries and requirement alignment documents
- Status dashboards and executive communications
- Stakeholder engagement plans and communication calendars

---

## Quality Assurance Lead

### Role Summary
Quality Assurance Leads define quality standards, oversee testing strategies, and validate that deliverables meet acceptance criteria and quality benchmarks. They ensure product reliability and customer satisfaction.

### Responsibilities
- Define quality standards and acceptance criteria for deliverables
- Develop and maintain testing strategies and test plans
- Oversee testing activities (unit, integration, system, UAT)
- Validate deliverables against requirements and quality benchmarks
- Identify, track, and prioritize quality issues and defects
- Provide quality metrics and trend reporting
- Collaborate on risk identification and mitigation related to quality

### Goals
- Ensure deliverables meet or exceed quality standards
- Reduce defect escape rates and post-release issues
- Drive continuous improvement in quality processes
- Maximize customer satisfaction and trust

### Interactions with Existing Roles
- **Collaborates with Developers**: Works on test strategy, provides test feedback, validates implementations
- **Reports to Project Manager**: Updates on quality metrics, identifies blockers, escalates critical issues
- **Works with Stakeholder Engagement Lead**: Communicates quality metrics and assurance to stakeholders
- **Advises Product Manager**: Provides quality-related trade-off analysis for prioritization

### Typical Communication
- Test plans and quality reports
- Defect tracking and metrics dashboards
- Quality reviews and release readiness assessments
- Quality standards documentation and best practices

---

## Documentation Owner

### Role Summary
Documentation Owners ensure that process documentation, project artifacts, and institutional knowledge remain current, accurate, and accessible to all team members. They support organizational learning and knowledge continuity.

### Responsibilities
- Maintain process documentation accuracy and completeness
- Ensure documentation reflects current practices and decisions
- Manage documentation version control and accessibility
- Coordinate documentation updates across project teams
- Create and maintain knowledge bases and reference materials
- Support onboarding and training through documentation
- Work with team members to capture and document lessons learned

### Goals
- Enable rapid onboarding and knowledge transfer
- Reduce redundant work and decision-making cycles
- Maintain a single source of truth for processes and practices
- Scale institutional knowledge across the organization

### Interactions with Existing Roles
- **Supports all roles**: Maintains documentation relevant to each persona and function
- **Works with Project Manager**: Documents processes, decisions, and project artifacts
- **Collaborates with Developers**: Captures technical documentation and architecture decisions
- **Partners with Retrospective facilitators**: Incorporates lessons learned into documentation

### Typical Communication
- Documentation updates and change notifications
- Knowledge base maintenance and access guidance
- Process documentation and procedure guides
- Onboarding materials and training resources

---

## Risk & Compliance Officer

### Role Summary
Risk & Compliance Officers identify, track, and manage project risks while ensuring compliance with organizational policies, regulatory requirements, and industry standards. They protect project viability and organizational integrity.

### Responsibilities
- Identify potential risks and compliance gaps throughout the project lifecycle
- Develop and maintain risk registers and mitigation strategies
- Monitor risk indicators and escalate emerging threats
- Ensure project activities comply with organizational and regulatory requirements
- Coordinate compliance audits and assessments
- Provide guidance on risk management best practices
- Escalate critical risks and compliance issues to leadership

### Goals
- Minimize project disruption from unforeseen risks
- Ensure full regulatory and policy compliance
- Protect organizational reputation and stakeholder trust
- Enable informed risk-based decision making

### Interactions with Existing Roles
- **Reports to Project Manager**: Escalates risks, provides risk assessments, updates risk registers
- **Coordinates with Developers**: Ensures technical decisions meet compliance requirements
- **Works with Quality Assurance Lead**: Identifies quality-related risks and mitigation strategies
- **Partners with Stakeholder Engagement Lead**: Communicates compliance and risk status to stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Risk registers and mitigation plans
- Compliance checklists and assessment reports
- Risk escalation notices and decision documentation
- Policy and compliance guidance communications

---

## Technical Lead/Architect

### Role Summary
Technical Leads and Architects provide technical guidance, review architectural and design decisions, and ensure best practices are followed. They balance technical excellence with project constraints and drive technical strategy.

### Responsibilities
- Define technical architecture and design patterns
- Review technical designs and code for compliance with standards
- Provide guidance on technology selection and technical trade-offs
- Identify and propose mitigation for technical risks
- Ensure adherence to coding standards and best practices
- Mentor team members on technical skills and approaches
- Collaborate on scalability, performance, and security considerations

### Goals
- Deliver technically sound, maintainable solutions
- Reduce technical debt and future rework
- Enable team growth and technical capability building
- Ensure solutions scale and perform under expected loads

### Interactions with Existing Roles
- **Advises Developers**: Provides technical guidance, reviews designs and code
- **Collaborates with Quality Assurance Lead**: Designs testable architectures, advises on technical testing approaches
- **Works with Project Manager**: Identifies technical risks and informs timeline/resource estimates
- **Partners with Product Manager**: Provides technical feasibility input on product decisions

### Typical Communication
- Technical design documents and architecture diagrams
- Code review feedback and technical guidance
- Technical risk assessments and mitigation strategies
- Architecture decision records and technical standards documentation
